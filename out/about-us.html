About

A published clinic schedule, open to the patients who can reach it.

HealthCare Service is a booking desk for clinics that already know their doctors. A doctor applies, an admin approves the license, and patients book a published slot.

Payment goes through bKash. A completed visit can carry one prescription PDF. There is no separate medical record beyond the appointment, the payment, and that file.

Core values

What the desk will and will not do.

Open windows
Published slots only
A patient only sees visits that are still free. Closed windows do not appear as bookable.
Who practices
Approval first
A doctor applies with a license. An admin approves them before a schedule can go live.
When it holds
Payment confirms the visit
Booking sends the patient to bKash. The appointment stays pending until that payment returns.
What remains
The file stays on the visit
A completed visit can hold one prescription PDF, on the appointment and in the patient's email.

Our journey

From an application to a prescription on the visit.

Apply

Leadership

Seats on the desk, not a named roster.

These are the responsibilities the product is built around. The site does not list individual staff.

MD
Medical desk
Owns which doctors are approved to publish a schedule, and what a completed visit is allowed to carry.
PE
Patient experience
Owns the path from an open slot to bKash and back, including where the prescription PDF lands.
CO
Clinic operations
Owns the day's windows: what a doctor publishes, and which 20-minute visits stay bookable.
